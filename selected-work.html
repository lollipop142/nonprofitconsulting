Case studies

Selected engagements

A few examples of the moments organizations were facing, and the work that helped them move forward.

01

Scaling for sustainable impact

Strategic advisor

The moment

A global safe-water organization had ambitious growth goals, and its systems and structures needed to catch up.

The work

Translated growth goals into stronger organizational systems, clearer accountability, and improved governance.

The result

Coordinated execution across multiple countries, with the structure in place to sustain growth.

02

Turning growth into organizational strength

Executive Director

The moment

An international development organization was growing fast and needed strategy and leadership to match.

The work

Strengthened strategy, fundraising, governance, and cross-border leadership through a period of significant growth.

The result

2×+staff and revenue
20×program reach
03

Guiding responsible organizational closure

Interim executive leader

The moment

A climate organization had decided to wind down and needed steady leadership to close with care.

The work

Stepped into interim executive leadership to manage financial stewardship, funder obligations, staff and board coordination, and risk.

The result

An orderly wind-down, with remaining assets transitioned responsibly.

04

Strengthening strategy and governance

Strategic partner

The moment

Community-rooted and international organizations needed clearer direction and stronger governance.

The work

Clarified strategic direction, strengthened governance, and aligned leadership teams.

The result

Practical plans for implementation that leadership could carry forward.
